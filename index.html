I am a Computer Science PhD student at the University of Illinois Chicago, advised by Prof. Bing Liu.

My research focuses on machine learning for reasoning problems—including mathematical and logical tasks—neuro-symbolic learning, and generalization beyond the training distribution. I am interested in Transformer-based architectures and, more broadly, continual learning and reinforcement learning.

Before beginning my PhD, I worked on large-scale mobile network systems, data analysis, and 5G infrastructure. This engineering background continues to shape how I think about reliable and deployable intelligent systems.

Selected Research

Current work · manuscript under anonymous review

Neuro-symbolic reasoning for length generalization

Exploring how a Transformer can predict fixed, local actions while an external symbolic executor constructs the next reasoning state. The goal is to make learned reasoning procedures less dependent on input length.

  • Reasoning tasks include arithmetic and symbolic sequence problems.
  • The model operates on bounded local context rather than the full state.
  • Technical details are intentionally limited while the review is active.

MSc research · Gazi University

Cooperative spectrum sensing for IoT using smart contracts

Research connecting cognitive radio, spectrum sensing, blockchain, IoT, and smart contracts; presented at the 3rd International Graduate Studies Congress in 2023.
